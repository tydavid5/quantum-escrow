A client and a freelancer do not trust each other to release payment on time.

Quantum Escrow holds the payment in a milestone escrow. The client funds it first, the freelancer can see the money is held, and payment releases only after a hybrid approval: a classical signature plus a real ML-DSA-87 post-quantum signature. A classical signature on its own is rejected, every time.

Flow

How it works

  1. 1Create. The client opens a milestone escrow with a title, a freelancer, and an amount in fUSD.
  2. 2Fund. The client funds it in demo mode with test tokens. On testnet this is a real fund call inside a scoped EVM window.
  3. 3Approve, hybrid. The client signs the approval message twice: once classically, and once with a fresh ML-DSA-87 keypair from @qorechain/pqc 0.2.3. The demo verifies that post-quantum signature in the browser.
  4. 4Release. After hybrid approval, payment releases to the freelancer and a receipt is issued. If the window closes with no approval, the client can refund.

Classical only approval is rejected on chain by QuantumEscrow.approveMilestone. The contract test suite proves it: 7 passing tests, including classical only rejection and hybrid success. The on chain post-quantum check in local tests is a deterministic stand in for the PQC Verify precompile. Live, the approval was verified with the real PQC Verify precompile 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000a01, and the full live flow is complete: escrow 0 released, contract balance 0 QOR, Nov 15 about 02:44 to 02:49 WAT.
